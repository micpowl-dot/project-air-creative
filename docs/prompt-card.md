## The 5 prompts

**1. Catch me up**
> Summarize this [thread / doc / meeting notes] in 5 bullets: the key points, any decisions made, and the action items with owners and due dates.

**2. Draft it for me**
> Write a first draft of this [email / Slack message / update] about [topic] for [audience]. Lead with the main point, keep it short, and flag anything I should double-check.

**3. Pressure-test my thinking**
> Here's my plan: [paste it]. Play devil's advocate. What are the 3 biggest risks or gaps, and what would you do about each?

**4. Explain it simply**
> Explain [topic / tool / error message] like I'm new to it. Then give me the first 3 steps to actually get started.

**5. Get me unstuck**
> I need to [task] but I'm not sure where to start. Ask me 2-3 questions first, then give me a template or outline I can build from.

---

**Tip (small print at the bottom):** Be specific and paste in your real context. The first answer is a starting point, not the final word, push back and ask follow-ups.
